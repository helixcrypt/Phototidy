PhotoTidy

Clean up your Mac photo library, privately.

SupportPrivacy Policy

PhotoTidy finds near-duplicate shots, blurry and accidental photos, old screenshots and photos of receipts in your Apple Photos library, and helps you keep the best and remove the rest. Everything runs on your Mac.

Get help

Inside the app, choose Help › PhotoTidy Help (⌘?) for a full guide to every feature.

Common questions

Can I undo a delete?
Yes. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Open Photos, choose Recently Deleted, select the photos and click Recover.
Does PhotoTidy upload my photos?
No. All analysis, including Apple Intelligence features, runs on your Mac. PhotoTidy has no servers and collects no data.
PhotoTidy finds no photos.
PhotoTidy sees the same library as the Photos app on this Mac. Make sure iCloud Photos is turned on in Photos › Settings › iCloud, and that you chose Full Access when PhotoTidy asked for permission (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos).
Groups mix different scenes, or miss obvious duplicates.
Adjust Similarity (lower is stricter) and Burst gap on the Similar tab. The ? buttons next to each setting explain them, and the Presets menu has good starting points.
Sharp photos are flagged as blurry.
Lower the Blur cutoff on the Blurry tab. Smooth subjects like sky, snow or fog can look blurry to the detector, which is why blurry photos start unmarked.
Will deleting here delete on my iPhone too?
Yes, if you use iCloud Photos. Deletions sync to all your devices, and can be recovered from Recently Deleted for 30 days.
What do I need?
macOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence features (the Ask bar's smarter understanding, keeper explanations and month summaries) need a Mac with Apple silicon and Apple Intelligence turned on; everything else works without it.
