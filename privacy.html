Privacy Policy

PhotoTidy for Mac · Effective October 24, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy
In short: PhotoTidy does not collect, store, share or sell any of your data. Your photos and everything PhotoTidy learns about them stay on your Mac.

What PhotoTidy accesses

  • Your photo library, with your permission, to find similar, blurry, dark and document photos. Photos are read on your Mac and are never uploaded or sent anywhere by PhotoTidy.
  • Files and folders you choose, only when you use Export and pick a folder.

How your photos are analyzed

PhotoTidy uses Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and, where available, Apple Intelligence through the Foundation Models framework). All of this processing happens on your Mac. PhotoTidy has no servers and makes no network requests of its own. If a photo is stored only in iCloud, macOS may download it from your iCloud account so it can be analyzed, just as the Photos app does.

What is stored on your Mac

To remember your progress, PhotoTidy saves a small file on your Mac containing Photos' internal identifiers for photos you've reviewed, which months you've scanned, and totals of photos deleted and space freed. It also saves your settings, such as tile size. This information never leaves your Mac. Deleting PhotoTidy, or choosing Reset Progress in the app, removes it.

Deleting photos

PhotoTidy only deletes photos when you click a Delete button and confirm. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs deletions to your other devices under Apple's own privacy policy.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use PhotoTidy with Siri or Shortcuts, those requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. PhotoTidy only receives the request and answers it on your Mac.

What PhotoTidy does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

PhotoTidy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
